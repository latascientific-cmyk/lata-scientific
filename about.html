Company profile

Two decades of glass built to hold the exact reaction you design.

Lata Scientific is a precision fabricator of borosilicate glassware, glass process equipment and hygienic fluid-transfer components — from a single bench flask to a full pilot plant.

Our story

From a small glass workshop to a trusted process partner.

What began as a bench-glass workshop has grown into a fabricator that laboratories and process plants standardise on. We never outsourced the craft — glass is formed, annealed, machined and tested under one roof, so every unit is traceable to the person who made it.

As our clients scaled from research to production, we scaled with them: adding jacketed reactors, sectional columns, and a full fluid-transfer line of hoses, valves and fittings — all held to the same standard as our first graduated flask.

  • In-house forming, annealing, machining and testing
  • Borosilicate 3.3, 316L stainless and virgin PTFE
  • Documented QC and certificates of conformance

Mission

To make precision apparatus that behaves exactly the way a method sheet says it should — so our clients can trust the equipment and focus on the chemistry.

Vision

To be the single supplier a lab or plant can plan a build around — for glass, for fluid transfer, and for anything custom in between.

Values

Right materials, honest lead times, and documentation that stands up to an audit. No shortcuts a buyer can't see.
