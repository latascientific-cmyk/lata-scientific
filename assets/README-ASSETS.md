# Where to drop your real assets

The site is built with clean placeholders. Drop your real files into these folders and
the pages will pick them up (filenames below are what the HTML currently points to — keep
these names, or tell me your names and I'll rewire).

## Product photos → `assets/img/products/`
Use clean, well-lit photos on a white/light background. Recommended: 1200×900px, JPG or WebP.
Expected filenames (rename yours to match, or send me a list):

- `laboratory-glassware.jpg`
- `reaction-assemblies.jpg`
- `distillation-units.jpg`
- `glass-columns.jpg`
- `condensers-cooling.jpg`
- `scrubber-systems.jpg`
- `ptfe-hose.jpg`
- `silicone-hose.jpg`
- `sanitary-valves.jpg`
- `sanitary-fittings.jpg`
- `filter-housing.jpg`
- `custom-fabrication.jpg`

## Hero / banner images → `assets/img/hero/`
Large, high-quality. Recommended: 1920×1080px+.
- `hero-1.jpg`, `hero-2.jpg`, `hero-3.jpg`

## Logo → `assets/img/logo/`
- `lata-logo.svg` (preferred) or `lata-logo.png` (transparent background)

## Spec sheets (PDF) → `assets/pdfs/`
One PDF per product, e.g. `ptfe-hose-spec.pdf`. These embed on the product detail pages.

Until real files are added, the site shows tasteful SVG placeholders so nothing looks broken.
