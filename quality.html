Quality policy

Precision you can document, not just claim.

Quality at Lata Scientific isn't a final inspection — it's controlled at every stage, from material selection to the certificate that ships with your order.

Material control

Borosilicate 3.3 glass, 316L stainless and virgin PTFE — verified on receipt, never substituted for a cheaper grade.

Standards alignment

Joints and fittings machined to ISO/DIN, 3A and ASME-BPE so parts interchange with your existing equipment.

Dimensional gauging

Bores, wall thickness and capacity gauged against the drawing and logged for every unit.

Anneal & strain check

Controlled-cycle annealing with polariscope inspection so glass won't fail cold in service.

Leak & vacuum testing

Every assembly tested against its method sheet before it is cleared for dispatch.

Conformance certificates

Test results documented and a certificate of conformance supplied with each order on request.
