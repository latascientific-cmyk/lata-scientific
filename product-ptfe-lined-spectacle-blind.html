Product overview

What it does, and where it fits.

A spectacle blind is a figure-of-eight plate with one solid disc and one open ring, bolted between two flanges. Slackening the joint and swinging the plate either isolates the line absolutely or opens it — and because the plate projects beyond the flange, anyone can see from the ground which state the line is in. That visual certainty is why spectacle blinds are specified for positive isolation before entry and maintenance work.

The lined version keeps a fluoropolymer face on the process side in both positions, so the isolation point does not become the corrosion point. It is offered NB 1" to 18" in thicknesses from 16 mm to 40 mm, with PTFE, PFA and FEP all marked applicable across the range.

Benefits

Why it earns its place in the line.

  • Positive, provable isolation for permit-to-work and vessel entry
  • Line status readable at a glance from outside the pipework
  • Outstanding corrosion resistance to aggressive organic and inorganic chemicals across a wide temperature range
  • Non-stick, non-wetting bore — low surface energy keeps product moving and simplifies cleaning
  • Chemically inert and pure; no additives, plasticisers, stabilisers or lubricants to contaminate the process fluid
  • The lowest coefficient of friction of any solid, reducing pressure drop and deposit build-up
