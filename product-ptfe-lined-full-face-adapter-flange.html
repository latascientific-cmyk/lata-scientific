Product overview

What it does, and where it fits.

A full face adapter flange carries the fluoropolymer across the whole flange face rather than just the raised face. That makes it the correct transition piece between a lined line and equipment with a flat-faced or dissimilar flange — a plastic vessel nozzle, an FRP line, a rubber-lined item — where a raised-face joint would concentrate load and crack the mating flange.

The flange is cut from IS 2062 plate or ASTM A 105 and drilled to ASME/ANSI B16.5 Class 150. PTFE, PFA and FEP are all marked applicable across the full NB 1/2" to 16" range, at the same thicknesses as the lined blind flange.

Features

Built into every unit.

  • Fluoropolymer facing across the full flange face, not just the raised face
  • PTFE, PFA or FEP available in every size NB 1/2" to 16"
  • IS 2062 plate or ASTM A 105 drilled to B16.5 Class 150
  • Thickness 12 mm at NB 1/2" to 36.6 mm at NB 16"
  • Class 300 drilling and stainless steel bodies on request

Benefits

Why it earns its place in the line.

  • Spreads bolt load evenly — safe against flat-faced plastic, FRP and lined equipment
  • Provides a clean transition between lined and unlined sections
  • Outstanding corrosion resistance to aggressive organic and inorganic chemicals across a wide temperature range
  • Non-stick, non-wetting bore — low surface energy keeps product moving and simplifies cleaning
  • Chemically inert and pure; no additives, plasticisers, stabilisers or lubricants to contaminate the process fluid
  • The lowest coefficient of friction of any solid, reducing pressure drop and deposit build-up

Applications & industries

Where it is specified.

Typical applications

  • Connecting lined pipework to FRP, PP or PVDF equipment
  • Transitions between lined and unlined sections of a line
  • Mating to flat-faced vessel and pump nozzles
  • Adapting to rubber-lined and glass-lined equipment flanges
