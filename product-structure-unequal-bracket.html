Product overview

What it does, and where it fits.

The unequal bracket joins two tubes of different nominal bore. The main socket takes the larger tube, NB; the second socket, NB1, takes the smaller. That is the usual arrangement where a heavy vertical or frame member has to pick up lighter secondary framing.

Four references cover the practical pairings: 25/15, 30/15, 40/25 and 50/25.

Cast iron and slidable, fixed with grub screws in common with the rest of the range.

Features

Built into every unit.

  • Joins two tubes of different bore
  • Four pairings — 25/15, 30/15, 40/25 and 50/25
  • Cast iron, fixed with grub screws
  • Provides a fixing point as well as a clamp

Benefits

Why it earns its place in the line.

  • Slidable on the tube and locked with grub screws — position is set on site, not in the shop
  • Provides flexibility for future modifications without any hammering or welding
  • Cast iron construction, matched to the galvanised tube sizes in the range
  • A structure can be extended, raised or re-spanned by moving fittings rather than rebuilding

Applications & industries

Where it is specified.

Typical applications

  • Building a rectangular tubular structure to carry a glass plant
  • Supporting glass columns, pipelines and vessels in compression
  • Framing heating mantles, baths and vessel holders
  • Modifying an existing structure as the process layout changes

Industries served

  • Chemical processing
  • Pharmaceutical
  • Fine chemicals & dyes
  • Distillation & solvent recovery
  • Water & effluent treatment
  • Agrochemicals
  • Research & pilot plant
