Product overview

What it does, and where it fits.

A sight glass in a heated line is the coldest point in that line. Product crystallises on the glass, condensation fogs it, and within a shift the operator can no longer see through it. A jacketed sight flow indicator solves that by circulating steam or hot fluid around the viewing chamber so the glass stays at process temperature.

This model has a cast stainless steel body and jacket in CF8, CF8M, CF3M or CG3M, two toughened borosilicate 3.3 or soda lime windows, and PTFE gaskets. Face-to-face runs from 160 mm at 25NB to 1400 mm at 400NB.

Custom bodies, alternative jacket materials and higher pressure ratings can be built to order, and the whole unit can be lined in FEP or PFA for aggressive service.

Features

Built into every unit.

  • Cast stainless body and jacket — CF8, CF8M, CF3M or CG3M
  • Two toughened borosilicate 3.3 or soda lime windows
  • 25NB to 400NB, face to face 160 mm to 1400 mm
  • FEP/PFA (PTFE) lined version available

Benefits

Why it earns its place in the line.

  • Glass stays clear on products that would crystallise or condense on a cold window
  • Large view area with easy maintenance and durable construction
  • Robust flanged body drops straight into an existing line
  • Glass and gaskets replaceable in situ without cutting the pipework
  • Mountable in any orientation

Applications & industries

Where it is specified.

Typical applications

  • Crystallising and high-melting-point products
  • Viscous fluids that need to be kept warm to remain visible
  • Steam-traced lines where the sight glass is the cold spot
  • Confirming that a line is actually flowing
  • Watching for colour change, cloudiness or phase separation

Industries served

  • Chemical processing
  • Pharmaceutical
  • Dairy & food
  • Water & effluent treatment
