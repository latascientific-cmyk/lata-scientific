Our process

Six controlled stages, from raw tube to tested unit.

Order matters here — each stage measures what the last one made.

01

Design & drawing

Your sketch, sample or P&ID becomes a dimensioned fabrication drawing, confirmed before the torch is lit.

02

Glass forming

Borosilicate tube is lathe-turned, blown and shaped by hand to the drawn geometry — walls even, bores true.

03

Joint machining

Cones, sockets, flanges and tri-clamp ferrules ground and lapped to ISO/DIN and ASME-BPE standards.

04

Hose crimping & assembly

PTFE and silicone hose cut, skived and crimped to fittings, then flow- and pressure-checked as an assembly.

05

Annealing & testing

Oven-annealed, polariscope-checked for strain, then leak- or vacuum-tested against the method sheet.

06

Packing & dispatch

Foam-cradled, crate-tested for export, dispatched with drawings and a certificate of conformance.
