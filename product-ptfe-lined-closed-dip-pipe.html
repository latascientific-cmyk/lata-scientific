Product overview

What it does, and where it fits.

A closed dip pipe is sealed at the bottom, so it forms a dry pocket reaching into the batch rather than a flow path. That makes it the housing for a temperature probe, a pH electrode or a level sensor that must read deep inside an aggressive batch without being wetted by it.

Because the pocket is closed, the instrument can be withdrawn and replaced while the vessel is in service. The pocket itself is lined with PTFE to ASTM D 1457 over an ASTM A 106 Gr. B body, in the same size range and lengths as the straight dip pipe.

A pH probe adapter and a thermowell with a tantalum plug are available on request, the latter for duties where even PTFE permeation would give a slow instrument response.

Benefits

Why it earns its place in the line.

  • Keeps sensitive instruments out of contact with an aggressive batch
  • Instrument replacement without vessel entry or shutdown
  • Outstanding corrosion resistance to aggressive organic and inorganic chemicals across a wide temperature range
  • Non-stick, non-wetting bore — low surface energy keeps product moving and simplifies cleaning
  • Chemically inert and pure; no additives, plasticisers, stabilisers or lubricants to contaminate the process fluid
  • The lowest coefficient of friction of any solid, reducing pressure drop and deposit build-up
